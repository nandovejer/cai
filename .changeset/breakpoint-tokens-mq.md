**Breaking (`@cai-ds/core`)**: the breakpoint tokens are renamed from `--cai-bp-*` to `--cai-mq-*` (`mq` for media query): `--cai-mq-sm`, `--cai-mq-md`, `--cai-mq-lg`, `--cai-mq-xl` and `--cai-mq-xxl` (was `--cai-bp-2xl`). The values do not change. Replace `--cai-bp-` with `--cai-mq-`, and `--cai-bp-2xl` with `--cai-mq-xxl`, wherever you read them.
