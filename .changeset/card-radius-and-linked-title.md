Card: rounded corners (`--cai-radius-md`, like figure and dropdown; the focus ring of a linked card follows them). The title of a linked card is in sans (`--cai-font-sans`), as a nav or a list of links is; a card without a link keeps the heading face. A linked card holds no other link or button: the stretched title link lies over them. For a card with actions, leave the title unlinked.
