New component: `<luz-timeline>`, a timeline around an ordered list (`ol.luz-timeline > li.luz-timeline__item` with a `time.luz-timeline__date`, a `.luz-timeline__title` and a `.luz-timeline__text`). Without JavaScript it is the list, with a line and a dot per item. With JavaScript, horizontal by default: a rail of date buttons under the current card, Previous and Next, the arrow keys, Home and End, and a swipe. `layout="vertical"` marks the item in the middle of the screen as active and the earlier ones as past (`data-luz-state`). Autoplay is opt-in (`autoplay`, `interval`), with a visible Pause / Play button, and never starts with reduced motion. `luz.js` loads the code only on a page that has a timeline (`dist/timeline.js`, its own size budget); `@luz-ds/core/timeline` defines the element on its own, without loading `luz.js`, for content added later or a page without the entry. Its strings (Previous, Next, Pause, Play; English and Spanish, overridden by `data-luz-label-*`) travel with the chunk; with `luz.js` on the page the entry passes its `t` through `initTimeline(root, translate)`, so a language added with `registerLocale()` reaches the timeline too. Each card has `aria-posinset` and `aria-setsize`; the focus moves to the new current date when the card it was in is hidden. `--luz-timeline-paper` on the element sets the colour the hump and the empty dots are painted with (for a timeline on a card). The styles are in `luz.css` and in `components/timeline.css`.
