**New: `<luz-mode>`, a color mode switcher with an Auto state.** Wrap core's color mode radios (`.luz-mode-switcher`, with or without `--cycle`) in `<luz-mode>` and add an "Auto" radio, first. Without JavaScript the radios change the mode through the tokens and Auto leaves the page light. With it, Auto follows the system (`prefers-contrast: more`, then `prefers-color-scheme: dark`, live) or, with `by="clock"`, the phase of the day from the local hour: `data-light` is `dawn`, `morning`, `afternoon`, `sunset`, `dusk` or `night` (dawn, dusk and night are dark), checked again at each minute (the page changes only when the phase does, so a `data-theme` set by hand stays until the next phase) and when the tab shows again, and `prefers-contrast: more` still wins. A chosen mode sets `data-theme`; with the clock its light is pinned (light: `afternoon`, dark and high contrast: `night`) and the minute does not touch it. `remember` stores the choice under core's `luz-theme` key and Auto removes it; without `remember` nothing is stored, and the key `luz.js` writes on a choice is removed. `for` names the element that gets the attributes (default `<html>`) and `light-attr` the attribute of the light (a `data-*` name, default `data-light`). A custom theme of `<html data-luz-themes>` is left to core. The element has `mode`, `light` and `preview(phase | hour | null)`, and dispatches `luz-mode:change`, bubbling, with `{ mode, theme, light, remembered, simulated }`. It writes no text. With `luz.js`, core's cycle button steps through the four states with no new code.

`site.min.js` loads its third chunk, `mode.min.js`, on idle when the page has a `<luz-mode>` (exported as `@luz-ds/site/mode`, with `phaseOf(hour)`); `dist/components/mode.css` is new. A page with full Subresource Integrity adds `mode.min.js` to the `integrity` section of its import map (SECURITY.md).
