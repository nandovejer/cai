New component: `.cai-pagination` (`components/pagination.css`), page links of a list that goes on over several pages. A `<nav aria-label>` around a `<ul>` of plain links: `.cai-pagination__link` (at least 44 px), `aria-current="page"` for the current one, `aria-disabled="true"` on a `<span>` for Previous or Next at either end, `.cai-pagination__gap` for the "…". No JavaScript. In forced colors the current page takes `Highlight`. Page: `/docs/components/pagination/`.
