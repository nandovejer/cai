New pattern: the site header, `.cai-platform-site-header` (`components/site-header.css`). The band at the top of every page: the site's name (`__brand`), its main links (`__nav`, a native `<nav popover>`) and a few controls (`__actions`: the search trigger, the language switcher, a button), in a wrapping row (`__inner`) with the gutters of `.o-container`. Below 769 px the links collapse into a panel under the header, opened by core's `.cai-nav-toggle` through `popovertarget`: no JavaScript, and the browser gives Escape, light dismiss, focus return and the expanded state. From 769 px they are a row. The current link takes `aria-current` and an underline. `--sticky` opts in to a sticky header, only in a window at least 30rem tall, and sets the page's `scroll-padding-block-start` to the larger of the header's height and `3.5em`, so a field reached with Tab is not left under it. `--cai-platform-site-header-h` (on `:root`, 53 px at the default text size) is where the panel and the language list open. The scrim of the open panel starts under the header, so the header keeps its contrast, in forced colors too. A panel left open when the window grows keeps its place under the header. No new token, no JavaScript: platform JS is unchanged. Platform CSS grows by about 0.4 kB (3.15 of 4.00 kB). Pages: `/docs/platform/site-header/`; Page shell, Page templates (a Content page template) and the class reference now use it.

**Known issues:** if you keep pressing Tab after the last link of the open panel, focus moves into the page and the panel stays open over it, hiding what is behind; Escape closes it (WCAG 2.4.12, level AAA; 2.4.11 AA is met). The panel opens under the header at its default-text height: with enlarged text and a header that wraps to a second row it can cover that row. Not yet reviewed in Safari and Firefox.
