The tokens ship in the W3C Design Tokens (DTCG) format, for Figma and other design tools.

**New (`@cai-ds/tokens`)**

- `@cai-ds/tokens/dtcg` → `dist/cai-tokens.dtcg.json`, generated by `pnpm tokens:build` from the same sources as the CSS (`tokens.json` and `src/semantic.css`), so it cannot drift from them.
- Two top-level groups, one per collection: `primitives` (one mode) and `semantic` (modes Light, Dark and High contrast, one per `data-theme` value). A semantic token's `$value` is its Light value; every mode is in `$extensions.mode`. Where the CSS uses a primitive (`var(--cai-gray-90)`), the token is an alias of it (`{primitives.gray.90}`).
- Name rule: drop `--cai-`; the first segment is the group, the rest is the name. `--cai-surface-page` is `surface/page`, `--cai-space-md` is `space/md`. `--cai-surface`, which is also a group, is `surface/$root`.
- Colours are sRGB objects with a `hex` fallback; sizes are `dimension` in `px` or `rem`; font stacks are `fontFamily` arrays; shadows are DTCG `shadow` values.

Custom themes (`minimalist`, `vejer`) are not in the file; they are not modes of the semantic collection.
