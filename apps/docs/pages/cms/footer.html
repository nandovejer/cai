Variants and options

There are no modifiers. The footer is a class of cms on a native <footer>, with a row of core's inside.

ClassEffect
.luz-cms-footer
On <footer>: a rule on top, 24 px above its row and 32 px below; its row spreads its children to the two ends, and is a column aligned to the start at 768 px and below
.o-container.o-container--inline.o-row (core)
The row: the width and gutters of the page, so its content starts where the main's does
.o-actions (core)
The links, as an action group

How it works

CSS from @luz-ds/cms on core's objects. No JavaScript, and @luz-ds/site is optional.

  • cms adds the rule, the room around the row and how the row spreads its children; .o-container--inline lines the row up with the page's content.
  • On an .o-page the main grows, so the footer rests on the bottom of a page shorter than the window.
  • The column at 768 px and below does not wrap, so its children stay one under the other.

Without JavaScript

Nothing changes: the footer is HTML and CSS. A color mode switcher inside it keeps working as radios.

Keyboard and ARIA

The footer has no interaction of its own; its links keep theirs.

  • A <footer> that is a direct child of <body> is the contentinfo landmark.
  • Name a <nav> of links with aria-label, and keep the links in the same order on every page.

Contrast and focus are in the Design tab.

Design

When to use

Use the footer at the end of every page of a blog or a newspaper, for who publishes it and a few secondary links: corrections, contact, the accessibility statement.

When not to use

Do not put the main navigation, or anything needed to finish a task, in the footer.

  • The sections go in the header.
  • A landing page has a footer of its own.

Do and don't

Writing the content

Keep it short: who publishes it, and two to four links.

  • Each link says where it goes: "Accessibility statement", not "Legal".

Contrast and focus

The footer adds only a top rule (--luz-divider), which is decorative; its text and links keep their own colours.

  • Focus: links and buttons in it keep their own ring.
  • Forced colors: the rule is a border, so it stays.

Tokens it uses

The 4 tokens that the .luz-cms-footer rules of cms's components/footer.css read, by family. Each family links to its page, with the values in every mode. In Figma they are variables with the same names: see Tokens in Figma.
