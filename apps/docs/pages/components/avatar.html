Example

AB CD EF

Known issues

None known.

When to use

To identify a person or account with initials where space is tight.

When not to use

When the name is already shown next to it and the avatar adds nothing: hide it from assistive technology instead.

How it works

A span with role="img" and an aria-label holding the full name, in three sizes.

Content guidance

Initials in capitals. The accessible name is the real name, not the initials.

Keyboard and ARIA

Not interactive.
