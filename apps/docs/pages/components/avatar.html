Variants and options

Pick one size modifier. Without one, the avatar has no size of its own.

ClassSize
.cai-avatar--sm
24 × 24 px, extra-small text
.cai-avatar--md
36 × 36 px, small text
.cai-avatar--lg
48 × 48 px, base text

How it works

An inline box that centres its text, styled by components/avatar.css. No JavaScript.

  • The initials are set in the mono font, in capitals (text-transform: uppercase).
  • The colours are the brand tint and the brand text colour, in every mode.

Without JavaScript

Nothing changes: the avatar is HTML and CSS only.

Keyboard and ARIA

An avatar is not interactive and takes no focus.

  • On its own, it is an image: role="img" and an aria-label with the full name, so a screen reader does not spell out the initials.
  • Next to the name, it is decoration: aria-hidden="true".
  • Inside a link or a button, the control's text names it; hide the avatar.

Writing the content is in the Design tab.

Design

When to use

Use an avatar to identify a person or an account where space is tight, such as a list of members or the author of a comment.

When not to use

Do not use an avatar as the only way to tell people apart.

  • Two people can share initials: show the name as text nearby.
  • For a status or a category, use a tag.

Writing the content

Two initials, from the name the person uses.

  • The accessible name is the full name, never the initials.
  • Use the same size for every avatar in one list.
