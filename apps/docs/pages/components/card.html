When to use

To group a small set of related facts that read as one unit.

When not to use

Do not nest cards. Do not make the whole card a click target with a script: link the title instead.

How it works

A container with a title and a body. Pick the heading level that fits the page outline.

Content guidance

A short noun title, then one or two lines. No full stops on titles.

Keyboard and ARIA

Not interactive. A link inside it follows the normal link rules.
