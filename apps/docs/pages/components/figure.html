Known issues

None known.

When to use

To show a chart, image or diagram with a caption.

When not to use

For decoration. A decorative image gets an empty alt and no figure.

How it works

A native figure with a figcaption. The media needs a text alternative; a chart needs its data as text or a table.

Content guidance

The caption says what the figure shows and for what period or scope.

Keyboard and ARIA

Not interactive.
