Known issues

Copy the markup

An audio player. The video player adds a .cai-player-media-wrap around the <video> and a mute button and volume slider: copy it from the example above.

HTML 
<div class="cai-player" data-type="audio" role="group" aria-label="Episode 12 player">
  <audio class="cai-player-audio" preload="none" controls>
    <source src="episode-12.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
  </audio>
  <div class="cai-player-controls cai-player-controls--audio">
    <button class="cai-player-btn cai-player-playpause" type="button" aria-label="Play">
      <svg class="icon-play" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="currentColor" aria-hidden="true"><polygon points="6,3 20,12 6,21"/></svg>
      <svg class="icon-pause" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="currentColor" aria-hidden="true" style="display:none"><rect x="5" y="3" width="4" height="18"/><rect x="15" y="3" width="4" height="18"/></svg>
    </button>
    <div class="cai-player-progress-row cai-player-progress-row--audio">
      <span class="cai-player-time cai-player-current">0:00</span>
      <input class="cai-player-seekbar" type="range" min="0" max="1000" step="1" value="0" aria-label="Seek audio">

</div>

How it works

The native media element plays the file; player.js drives the CAI controls, styled by components/player.css.

  • initPlayers() mounts every .cai-player on the page. cai.js runs it.
  • On mount, the script removes the native controls and binds the CAI ones: play and pause, time, seek and volume.
  • Seek and volume are native <input type="range"> sliders.
  • mountPlayer(root) and mountMidiPlayer(root) mount one player.

Without JavaScript

Audio and video show the browser's own controls, and the CAI controls stay hidden. A MIDI player shows its fallback text instead.

Keyboard and ARIA

Tab moves through the controls; each one keeps its native keys.

  • Buttons: Enter or Space. Their names change with the state: "Play" or "Pause", "Mute" or "Unmute".
  • Sliders: arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down. The seek slider says the time as text.
  • When the player itself has focus, not one of its controls (after a click on the video, for example): Space or K plays and pauses, and M mutes. F goes full screen when the player has a full-screen button.
  • The player is a role="group" named by its aria-label.

Design

When to use

Use a player to play a video, a sound or a MIDI file inside the page, when the reader starts it.

When not to use

Do not use a player for media that starts on its own or plays in the background.

  • Never autoplay.
  • For a short animated illustration with no sound, use an image or a figure.

Writing the content

Name each player by what it plays: "Episode 12 player", not "Audio player".

  • Give video captions (<track kind="captions">) and a transcript when it has speech.
  • Describe a silent video in its aria-label or next to it.
  • Say the length and the format next to the player when it is large.
