Known issues

Captions and audio description depend on the media you provide. MIDI has no equivalent without JavaScript.

When to use

To play video, audio or a MIDI file in the page.

When not to use

Do not autoplay. Do not use it for background media.

How it works

Without JavaScript the native controls of the audio and video element are shown. The script replaces them with the CAI controls; seek and volume are native range inputs. MIDI needs JavaScript and Web Audio.

Content guidance

Name each player with aria-label and give video a text alternative or a transcript.

Keyboard and ARIA

Space or K plays and pauses, M mutes, F goes full screen on video. The sliders take arrow keys, Home, End and Page Up and Down.
