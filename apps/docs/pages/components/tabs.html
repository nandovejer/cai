Code

How it works

Without JavaScript: links to panels that are all visible. With it: the ARIA tabs pattern, added by the script, with roving tabindex.

Keyboard and ARIA

Tab moves into the tab list; arrow keys move between tabs; Home and End go to the first and last; Space selects the focused tab; Tab leaves to the panel. In right-to-left text, Left and Right follow the reading direction. A link to a section inside a hidden panel opens its tab, and find in page reaches the hidden panels in browsers that support hidden="until-found".

Writing the content is in the Design tab.

Design

When to use

To switch between panels of the same level that the reader will not compare side by side.

When not to use

For navigation between pages, or for content the reader needs all at once.
