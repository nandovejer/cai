Arrow keys, Home and End move between tabs.

Known issues

None known.

When to use

To switch between panels of the same level that the reader will not compare side by side.

When not to use

For navigation between pages, or for content the reader needs all at once.

How it works

Without JavaScript: links to panels that are all visible. With it: the ARIA tabs pattern, added by the script, with roving tabindex.

Content guidance

Labels are one or two words that name the panel.

Keyboard and ARIA

Tab moves into the tab list; arrow keys move between tabs; Home and End go to the first and last; Tab leaves to the panel.
