A static specimen: CAI draws the toast, and you place it, show it and hide it.

Known issues

CAI ships the look only. The live region, the placement, the timing and the close button's behaviour are yours to build, and to make accessible.

Code Design

Code

Copy the markup

Keep an empty live region on the page, and put the toast inside it when the result happens.

HTML 
<div role="status">
  <div class="cai-toast">
    <div class="cai-toast__content">
      <div class="cai-toast__kind">Saved</div>
      <div class="cai-toast__msg">Draft saved.</div>
    </div>
    <button class="cai-toast__close" type="button" aria-label="Dismiss notification">×</button>
  </div>
</div>

Variants and options

There are no modifiers.

ClassWhat it does
.cai-toast
A raised box on the strong surface, 320 px wide at most and never wider than its container
.cai-toast__kind

.cai-toast__msg
The message
.cai-toast__close
The × button, 28 px at least

How it works

A styled block from components/toast.css. CAI has no toast script.

  • Position it with your own CSS, for example fixed at the bottom of the screen, above the page with --cai-z-toast.
  • If it hides by itself, leave it on screen for at least 5 seconds and keep it while it has focus or the pointer is over it.
  • The close button needs your script to remove the toast.

Without JavaScript

A toast only appears in answer to something your script did, so it does not exist without JavaScript. Show the same result on the page, for example in an alert after a form is sent.

Keyboard and ARIA

Showing a toast does not move focus.

  • Insert it into a polite live region (role="status") that is already on the page, so screen readers announce it.
  • The close button is reachable with Tab, and named "Dismiss notification".

Writing the content is in the Design tab.

Design

When to use

Use a toast to confirm a small result that needs no answer: "Draft saved", "Link copied".

When not to use

Do not use a toast for anything the reader must act on or must not miss: it goes away.

  • For an error, use an alert next to what failed.
  • For a decision, use a modal.
  • Do not put links or buttons in it, other than the close button.

Writing the content

One short line, in the past tense: "File exported."

  • The kind label is one word: "Saved", "Success".
  • Do not repeat what the page already shows.
