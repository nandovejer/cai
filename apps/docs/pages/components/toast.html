Known issues

CAI ships the look only: the live region and the timer are the consumer's job.

When to use

To confirm a low-stakes result that needs no reply, such as "Draft saved".

When not to use

For errors the reader must act on, or anything they might miss: toasts disappear.

How it works

A styled block with a close button. CAI leaves placement and timing to you; if it fades, give it at least 5 seconds and let it be paused.

Content guidance

Past tense, one short line. No actions inside it.

Keyboard and ARIA

The close button is reachable with Tab. Announce the toast in a polite live region.
