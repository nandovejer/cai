Example

Hides the project from the list

Point at the button, or focus it with Tab.

Known issues

Partly fails WCAG 1.4.13 (content on hover or focus): Escape does not close the tooltip, so the reader has to move the pointer or the focus away. Keep tooltips short and non-essential. Listed in the accessibility statement.

How it works

CSS only, from components/tooltip.css: the tip shows while the wrapper is hovered or holds focus.

  • It stays open while the pointer moves from the control onto the tip.
  • It fades in and out over --cai-duration-fast, and appears at once under reduced motion.
  • The tip does not wrap: keep it short, or it can overflow a narrow screen.

Without JavaScript

Nothing changes: the tooltip has no script.

Keyboard and ARIA

Focus the control with Tab and the tip shows; move the focus away and it hides.

  • aria-describedby on the control points at the tip, so screen readers read it after the control's name.
  • The tip has role="tooltip" and never takes focus.
  • The control keeps its own name: the tip is a description, not a label.

Contrast and focus are in the Design tab.

Design

When to use

Use a tooltip to add a short explanation to a control whose label is clear but brief.

When not to use

Do not put anything essential, long or interactive in a tooltip.

  • Touch screens have no hover: put information people need on the page, as a hint (form fields).
  • Never use a tooltip as the only name of an icon button.
  • No links or buttons inside it.

Do and don't

Writing the content

One short phrase that says what the control does or why: "Hides the project from the list".

  • Do not repeat the control's label.
  • No full stop on a fragment.

Measured from the token values of each mode, rounded down. Needs is the WCAG 2.2 AA minimum: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for focus rings, borders and icons.

  • Focus: the tooltip shows when its control has focus, not only on hover; the ring is the control's own.
  • Edges: a border and a shadow keep it apart from what is under it, in forced colors too.
