Variants and options

No modifiers: two parts, alone or inside a card.

ClassWhat it does
.luz-landing-stat
The block: the value over the label, a small gap (--luz-space-2xs) apart, and no padding or surface
.luz-landing-stat__value
The figure: heading font, 3xl, light, with figures of equal width; it wraps anywhere in a narrow column
.luz-landing-stat__label
What the figure counts: sm size, in the secondary text colour

How it works

CSS from @luz-ds/landing. No JavaScript, and @luz-ds/site is optional.

  • The stat lays out nothing: put several in a core grid list, ul.o-grid.o-grid--3. In a card it uses the card's padding and surface.

Without JavaScript

Nothing changes: the stat is HTML and CSS, and does not count up.

Keyboard and ARIA

The stat has no interaction or role of its own.

  • The value comes before the label in the source, so it is read in that order. Put stats in a <ul>, under the section's heading.

Contrast and focus are in the Design tab.

Design

When to use

Use a stat for a figure that backs up a claim: how many use the product, how fast it is. Two to four together read at a glance.

When not to use

Do not use a stat for a figure that needs a sentence to be understood.

  • For rows and columns to compare, use a table.

Do and don't

Writing the content

The value is a number with its unit, in five characters or fewer if you can. The label says what the number counts.

  • Write the figure the way you would say it: "4,000+", "99.95%", "12 min".
  • The label is lower case, without a full stop, and says over what period or for whom: "uptime in the last year".
  • Say where a figure comes from, in a note under the section, if a reader could ask.
