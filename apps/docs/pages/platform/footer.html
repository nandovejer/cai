ClassEffect
.cai-platform-footer
A rule on top; its children spread along one row, and stack at 768 px and below

How it works

A flex row from @cai-ds/platform on a <footer>. No JavaScript.

  • Add .o-container to line it up with the content.
  • Group the links in an action group.

Without JavaScript

Nothing changes: the footer is HTML and CSS. A color mode switcher inside it keeps working as radios.

Keyboard and ARIA

The footer has no interaction of its own; its links keep theirs.

  • A <footer> that is a direct child of <body> is the contentinfo landmark.
  • Keep the links in the same order on every page.

Writing the content is in the Design tab.

Design

When to use

Use a footer at the end of every page, for who made the site, its licence and a few secondary links.

When not to use

Do not put the main navigation, or anything needed to finish a task, in the footer.

  • Main navigation goes in the header or a sidebar.

Writing the content

Keep it short: who made it, the licence, and two to four links.

  • Each link says where it goes: "Accessibility statement", not "Legal".

Keyboard and ARIA are in the Code tab.
