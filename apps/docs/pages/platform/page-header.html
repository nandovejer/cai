<div class="cai-platform-page-header">
  <h1 class="cai-platform-page-header__title">Billing</h1>
  <p class="cai-platform-page-header__lead">Invoices, payment methods and plan limits for your workspace.</p>
</div>

Variants and options

There are no modifiers. The lead is optional.

ClassWhat it does
.cai-platform-page-header
The block, with 32 px of space below it
.cai-platform-page-header__title
The title: heading font, 3xl size, light weight; 2xl at 480 px and below
.cai-platform-page-header__lead
One paragraph in the secondary text colour, 600 px wide at most

How it works

A wrapper with a title and a lead, styled by @cai-ds/platform. No JavaScript.

  • The classes change the look only. The title must be the page's <h1>.

Without JavaScript

Nothing changes: the page header is HTML and CSS.

Keyboard and ARIA

The page header has no interaction.

  • An <h1> title lets screen reader users find the page title from the heading list.
  • Keep it inside <main>, so the skip link lands right before it.

Writing the content is in the Design tab.

Design

When to use

Use a page header at the top of a page or a main view, to give its title and one sentence about what it is for.

When not to use

Do not use it for a section inside the page: use a plain heading of the right level.

  • One page header per page.

Writing the content

The title names the page in a few words, in sentence case.

  • The lead is one sentence, under 25 words, that says what the reader can do here.
  • Match the title to the link that led here and to the start of <title>.

Keyboard and ARIA are in the Code tab.
