Modes and themes

A color mode changes how light or dark the page is; a theme changes its whole identity. CAI ships one look, in three modes. A theme of your own sits on top of it.

  • The three modes ship with the tokens: data-theme="light", "dark" and "high-contrast". Light is the default.
  • The default look is Vejer, inspired by Vejer de la Frontera: ET Book for running text, whitewash and sandstone surfaces, iron-black text, sky-blue links and actions, and every link underlined.
  • CAI ships no other theme. Make your own: copy the template, change what you need, and check it.
<html data-theme="dark">
<html data-theme="high-contrast">
<html data-theme="my-brand" data-mode="dark">

Every mode is measured by pnpm check:contrast: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for disabled text, borders and focus rings. The same measure runs on a theme of your own with the contrast check.
