The higher the surface, the larger the shadow. Never use a shadow as the only edge of a surface: pair it with a border, because shadows disappear in forced colors. Shadows are the same in every mode.

  • --luz-shadow-sm: a card under the pointer.
  • --luz-shadow-md: tooltips and toasts.
  • --luz-shadow-lg: dialogs and the drawer.
  • --luz-shadow-icon: an icon over a picture, such as the play button on a video.
--luz-shadow-sm

--luz-shadow-md

--luz-shadow-lg

--luz-shadow-icon
0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.5)
