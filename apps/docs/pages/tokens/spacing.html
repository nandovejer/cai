44px is the WCAG 2.5.5 target size; the other steps are about 1.25× apart on the 4px grid.

  • --cai-control-sm: close buttons and other small icon targets.
  • --cai-control-md: small buttons.
  • --cai-control-lg: buttons and fields, the default.
  • --cai-control-xl: large buttons.
